## 🧠 Investment Thesis

The position in Micron was built during a period of clear weakness in the semiconductor sector, particularly in the memory segment (DRAM/NAND).

The assumption was cyclical:

- MU's business is extremely cyclical
- memory prices are subject to sharp fluctuations
- the market tends to overprice both peaks and troughs

The goal was to enter during the sentiment-depression phase and hold until improvement materialized.

---

## 📉 Why I Bought

### ✅ 1️⃣ Memory cyclicality

Micron is heavily dependent on:

- DRAM prices  
- NAND prices  
- demand for electronics  

👉 which leads to a highly predictable pattern:

boom → oversupply → crash → recovery

---

### ✅ 2️⃣ Sector weakness (2022–2023)

At the time of purchase:

- demand for PCs and smartphones was falling  
- memory prices were declining  
- sentiment was deeply negative

---

### ✅ 3️⃣ Scaling in over time (DCA)

Instead of a single entry:

- 2022: early entry
- 2023: added closer to the bottom

👉 The result:

- improved average price  
- reduced timing risk  

---

## 📊 What Happened Along the Way

In 2023 and 2024:

- the semiconductor sector began to recover  
- demand outlook improved  
- AI fueled increased interest in memory and infrastructure  

👉 The market began:

repricing toward higher expectations

---

## 💰 Why I Sold

### ✅ 1️⃣ Valuation normalization

- the stock moved from "underpricing" to "fair value / optimism"  
- sentiment had improved significantly  

---

### ✅ 2️⃣ Risk/Reward

After an ~80% gain:

the asymmetry was no longer attractive

---

### ✅ 3️⃣ The nature of the trade

This was:

> **a cyclical position, not a structural one**

which means:

buy the dip → sell the bounce

---

## 🧠 What Went Well

- correctly calling the cycle ✅  
- scaling in over time ✅  
- patience ✅  
- taking advantage of sentiment ✅  

---

## ⚠️ What Could Have Been Better

- taking partial profits instead of a full exit  
- larger exposure at the bottom (more aggressive sizing)  

---

## 🔥 Key Takeaways

- semiconductors (especially memory) are extremely cyclical
- the best opportunities appear during peak pessimism
- DCA works very well in cyclical sectors
- proper positioning matters more than perfect timing

---

## 📊 Summary

This trade was a classic example of:

buy crisis → hold recovery → sell normalization

👉 demonstrating:

- the importance of the cycle  
- the role of patience  
- the edge of a structural approach over market timing
